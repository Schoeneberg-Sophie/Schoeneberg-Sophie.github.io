About Me

Hello! I'm Sophie Schöneberg, a Data Science Master's student at the University of Mannheim with a strong focus on corporate security management and data science.

Background & Focus

I completed my bachelor's degree in Political Science at the University of Mannheim, where I focused on International Relations and Statistics with excellent grades. My work centered on large-scale data projects, such as analyzing sentiment differences in independent and state-controlled news articles, where I examined over 20,000 articles and their commentary across various economic, social, and political events. I primarily work with R and Python to develop the projects available on my page. I wrote my Bachelor's Thesis on the growing impact of dual-use satellites in space and the dangers that come with blurring the lines between civil and military objects.

I also work as a Student Researcher at the Chair of International Relations, where I collect information on pro-government militias, paramilitaries, and private security companies for various countries, with a primary focus on Africa and Asia. If you are interested in learning more about me, feel free to download my resume or message me on your preferred communication channel.

Interests

My interests lie in both qualitative and quantitative analyses of security-related events. With my skill set, I aim to provide a fresh perspective on the growing field of data science within corporate security environments. Backed by my academic and professional background, I am motivated by contemporary "breaking news" issues and their impacts on individuals and businesses. Here are a few questions I find compelling:

• Did the electricity supply get overwhelmed during the World Cup?
• Could James Bond traverse London without being detected by CCTV?
• How can we reliably distinguish between hateful bots and hateful people online?
